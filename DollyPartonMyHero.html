Why Dolly Parton is my hero

From a very young age I was introduced to country music.

My mum used to even play Randy Travis to me while I was in the womb!
Having a country music singer like Dolly was so refreshing because the genre was very male dominated.
Despite her looks and being called a Barbie, Dolly is a real, down to earth, smart business woman from humbling beginnings.

Knowing the upbringing Dolly had and her drive to succeed was always something I admired.
Even from a very young age she knew what she wanted to do.

During the Women’s right movement in the 1960s, Dolly was breaking the mould and singing songs like “Just Because I’m A Woman”,
even though it was withheld by radio stations at the time.

For me, if I ever feel stressed, low or need to get myself motivated – I just have to listen to Dolly and everything is alright!
